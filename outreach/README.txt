# Outreach Folder — Quick Reference

This folder contains everything you need to reach out to new leads and manage deals. Here is what each file is, when to use it, and what to change before sending.

---

## cold-email-1.md

**What it is:** A cold email for businesses with no website.
**When to use it:** You have found a local business through Google, a Facebook group, a directory, or word of mouth and they have no website at all.
**What to change:** Replace [Business Name], [First Name], and [Town/City] with the real details. Personalise the opening line if you can. The more specific you are, the better it performs.

---

## cold-email-2.md

**What it is:** A follow up for people who did not reply to cold-email-1 or cold-email-3.
**When to use it:** Send this 4 to 7 days after the first email with no reply. Send it as a reply to your original email so it sits in the same thread.
**What to change:** Replace [First Name]. Nothing else needs to change.

---

## cold-email-3.md

**What it is:** A cold email for businesses that have a website but it is weak, outdated, or broken.
**When to use it:** You have checked their website and spotted real problems. This works best when you can name a specific issue in the email rather than keeping it vague.
**What to change:** Replace [Business Name] and [First Name]. Fill in the specific issue you found in the bracketed line. Be concrete. Generic feedback gets ignored.

---

## facebook-dm.md

**What it is:** A short opening message for Facebook Messenger.
**When to use it:** You have found a local business on Facebook and want to start a conversation without coming across as a marketer.
**What to change:** Replace [First Name] and [Business Name]. Keep the message exactly as written. Do not add links or a pitch in the first message.

---

## instagram-dm.md

**What it is:** A short opening message for Instagram DMs.
**When to use it:** You have found a local business on Instagram. Works best for businesses that are active on Instagram but have a weak website or no website.
**What to change:** Replace [Business Name]. Keep it short. Instagram DMs feel weird if they are too long.

---

## proposal-template.md

**What it is:** A full proposal to send after a discovery call.
**When to use it:** You have spoken to a prospect, done their audit, and they are interested in moving forward.
**What to change:** Fill in every bracketed field before sending. The audit findings section is the most important part. Be specific. Vague findings make the proposal feel generic. Choose the one or two options that make the most sense for them and lead with those. You do not need to show all pricing options every time.

---

## crm-tracker.md

**What it is:** A guide for setting up your Google Sheets CRM.
**When to use it:** When setting up the sheet for the first time or onboarding someone new to the system.
**Notes:** Set this up once and maintain it consistently. The commission calculation only works if the Deals sheet is kept up to date. At the end of each month, update Sheet 3 before any commission is paid out. When Conner hits a new retainer tier, update all his active self-sourced retainer rows in the Deals sheet to the new rate.

---

## Notes on Tone

All copy in this folder follows the same rules. No dashes. No filler phrases. Short sentences. Plain English. Read it out loud before you send it. If it sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.

The goal is to sound like a real person who has actually looked at their business, not a template blasted out to a list.
