# CRM Tracker — Google Sheets Setup Guide

This sheet tracks every lead, where it came from, what they bought, and what commission is owed. Keep it updated after every deal closes.

---

## Sheet 1: Lead Tracker

| Column | Header | What Goes Here |
|---|---|---|
| A | Lead ID | A simple number, e.g. 001, 002. Add one for each new lead. |
| B | Business Name | The name of the business you are contacting. |
| C | Contact Name | First name of the person you spoke to. |
| D | Email | Their email address. |
| E | Phone | Their phone number if you have it. |
| F | Location | Town or city. |
| G | Lead Source | Either "Owner" (passed by the owner) or "Conner" (found by Conner). |
| H | Date Added | The date you first added this lead. Format: DD/MM/YYYY. |
| I | Status | Use one of: Contacted, Follow Up, Call Booked, Proposal Sent, Closed Won, Closed Lost, No Response. |
| J | Notes | Any details from calls, emails, or conversations. Keep it brief. |

---

## Sheet 2: Deals

Log a row here every time a deal closes.

| Column | Header | What Goes Here |
|---|---|---|
| A | Deal ID | Match to Lead ID from Sheet 1. |
| B | Business Name | Same as Sheet 1. |
| C | Lead Source | "Owner" or "Conner". |
| D | Deal Type | "One Off" or "Monthly Retainer". |
| E | Service | e.g. Growth Website, Ads Management, Pro Full Service. |
| F | Deal Value | The amount charged. One off: full amount. Retainer: monthly amount. |
| G | Date Closed | When the deal was signed or payment received. |
| H | Commission Rate | Calculated automatically using the formula in column I. |
| I | Commission Owed | Formula calculates based on source, deal type, and value (see rules below). |
| J | Month | For retainers, the month this commission relates to. Format: MM/YYYY. |
| K | Paid Out | Yes or No. Mark Yes once commission has been paid. |

---

## Commission Rules

### Owner Sourced Leads (passed by the owner to Conner)

**Rate: 20% of everything, forever.**

This applies to both one off payments and monthly retainers. As long as that client is paying, the owner earns 20%.

### Conner Sourced Leads — One Off Deals

| Deal Value | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| Under £500 | 40% |
| £500 and over | 30% |

### Conner Sourced Leads — Monthly Retainers

Commission rate depends on how many monthly retainer clients Conner has personally closed in total. This is a running total across all time, not just the current month.

| Conner's Total Monthly Clients Closed | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 | 20% |
| 6 to 10 | 25% |
| 11 to 20 | 30% |
| Over 20 | 40% |

**Important:** When Conner hits a new tier, the new rate applies to all of his existing self-sourced monthly clients immediately. Every retainer he sourced moves up with him.

---

## Sheet 3: Monthly Commission Summary

Use this sheet at the end of each month to calculate what is owed.

| Column | Header | What Goes Here |
|---|---|---|
| A | Month | The month being calculated. Format: MM/YYYY. |
| B | Conner Total Monthly Clients | Count of all active self-sourced retainer clients as of this month. Use this to determine Conner's tier. |
| C | Owner Commission Total | Sum of all 20% commission on owner-sourced deals active this month, both one offs and retainers. |
| D | Conner Commission Total | Sum of all Conner's commission using the correct tier rate for this month. |
| E | Total Paid Out | What has actually been paid this month. |
| F | Outstanding | What is still owed. Formula: (C + D) minus E. |

---

## Tips

Keep the Deals sheet clean. One row per deal per month for retainers so you can track ongoing payments accurately.

Colour code the Status column in the Lead Tracker. Use green for Closed Won, red for Closed Lost, yellow for active conversations. It makes it faster to scan.

Set up a filter on the Deals sheet so you can quickly view all rows for a given month or source.

When Conner hits a new tier, update the commission rate on all his active retainer rows in the Deals sheet for that month and going forward.
